# Sources: Kestrel Roofing Company

Modeled on **H.C. Anderson Roofing Company, Inc.**, a union commercial roofing contractor in the Rockford, Illinois area, from its Regulation Crowdfunding filings on SEC EDGAR (CIK 2078579). Construction payment mechanics (pay applications, retainage, lien waivers) are standard industry practice. The world keeps the real company's age, trade, market, revenue, debts and balance-sheet change, and invents the name, people, account numbers, customers and every dated event.

## Inferred, not stated in the filing

- **Closing date.** The filing does not date the sale. The world closes it on 2024-05-01 because the 10-year term loan matures on 2034-05-01 and a note to a founding-family member appears in the same table.
- **What the related-party receivable is.** The filing says only that $4,164,278 is due from a related party. The world reads it as cash advanced to the buyer's holding company, starting with the loan proceeds at closing.
