The world keeps the real company's structure, products, revenue mix, regions, fee and cost lines, the 2021 business email compromise, the call-back control that followed it, and its material weakness. ## Mechanics checked against published rules

- Apple pays within 45 days of the last day of the fiscal month, to the bank account and currency on file, one payment per currency per fiscal month, with regions below the threshold carried forward: [App Store Connect Help, overview of receiving payments](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments). Typical timing of about 33 days, and fiscal months of four or five weeks (one 35-day and two 28-day months per quarter): [RevenueCat, App Store fiscal calendar and payment dates](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/app-store-fiscal-calendar-and-payment-dates).
- Google pays orders processed in a calendar month around the 15th of the next month, net of that month's refunds and chargebacks: [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/137997?hl=en), [Google Payments Center](https://support.google.com/paymentscenter/answer/7159355).

## Inferred

- The fiscal month dates (December 1-28, 2024; December 29-February 1; February 2-March 1; March 2-29). They come from Apple's own quarter ends (December 28, 2024 and March 29, 2025) with five weeks in the first month of each quarter. Check them against App Store Connect's payment calendar before relying on exact days.
- The Delaware company as the group's US bank and payer of US crews. The filing has US filming and a US subsidiary but does not say which entity pays.
- That the parent bought the remaining 40% of FunVerse during 2024: it was 60% owned in the 2023 10-K/A and described as wholly owned in the 2025 20-F.
- The quarter's size (about $5.8 million paid by viewers). This is the 2025 in-app total spread over the year, a little above the average quarter.

## Known differences and oddities in the source

- The 20-F's foreign-exchange risk text says Singapore operations are in SGD, which is "not freely convertible", with rates "quoted by the PBOC" (China's central bank). This looks like wording copied from a Chinese issuer's template. SGD is freely convertible. The world keeps SGD books for the Singapore company but lets money convert freely.
- The 20-F gives aggregate cash paid to executive officers in 2025 as $101.6 million, which cannot be squared with the salaries it lists. It is not used.
- The chairman's name appears in two spellings, and the listing venue appears as both Nasdaq and NYSE American. Neither matters to the world.
