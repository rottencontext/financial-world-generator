# Sources: Foghorn Alley Brewing

Modeled on **Black Hammer Brewing LLC**, a taproom brewery in San Francisco, from its Regulation Crowdfunding filings on SEC EDGAR (CIK 1807883). The world keeps the real company's scale, revenue mix, debts and balance-sheet oddities, and invents the name, people, account numbers and day-to-day events.

## Filings used

| Document | What it gave |
|---|---|
| [Form C, October 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807883/000180788324000001/formcbhbc.pdf) | Organized January 2014 in California; 31 employees; FY22 and FY23 totals (revenue $1,026,481 and $1,173,515, net income -$218,795 and -$224,841, cash $77,476 and $6,722, receivables $16,970 and $86,417); cash -$20,313 by October 2024; the debt table (June 2020 community bonds of $100,000 at 9% to June 2025, a $650,000 EIDL from January 2022 at 3.75% to 2052, a 0% line of credit drawn since May 2019); the bond platform's $25 monthly processing fee |
| [2023 financial statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807883/000180788324000001/2023combinedfinancials.pdf) | The full 2023 profit and loss (every revenue and expense line used in section 1 of the prompt, including tips of $122,770, the SF health surcharge, service charges, event income, wholesale bottles and kegs, work-in-progress loss of $19,492, cash over/short of $4,124); the balance sheet at 2023-12-31 (Bank of America accounts including one named "DO NOT USE FOR CHECKS" and a PPP account, several Chase card numbers marked deleted, gift card liability $24,284, keg deposits $13,745, can and bottle deposits, CalSavers, loans from a founder's family member, the CEO, WebBank (Intuit) and US Bank) |
| Other years on the same [EDGAR company page](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1807883&type=C) | 2020 Form C, 2018-2019 cash flows, 2022 and 2024 statements, for a world set in another year |

## Invented for this world

The company name, every person, the account numbers, the payroll schedule, the specific customers and vendors, and every dated event in section 6 (the dumped batch's cause, the card fraud, the wrong checkbook, the restaurant that closes). The world also assumes the EIDL is still in deferment during 2023 and that the brewery self-distributes, which fit the filing but are not stated in it.

## Known differences from the filing

The 2023 balance sheet carries the EIDL at $500,000 while the Form C debt table says $650,000 outstanding. The prompt uses the Form C figure.
