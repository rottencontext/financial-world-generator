# Sources: Hollow Oak Cookie Co.

Modeled on **Legally Addictive Foods, Inc.**, a Weaverville, North Carolina maker of sweet-and-salty cookies, from its Regulation Crowdfunding filing on SEC EDGAR (CIK 2142533). The world keeps the real company's size, channels, named accounts, 2025 revenue, margins, working capital and debt stack. The name, people and account numbers are invented, and so is every system the company uses, every document and email, and every dated event. The distributor's deduction codes and dispute windows come from a published guide to UNFI's supplier rules.

This target is built for accounts-receivable and accounts-payable agents such as FullSeam's: invoices, POs, receiving records, remittances, portal statuses and email are records here, not just the money.

## Mechanics checked against published rules

- UNFI deduction codes: `[Invoice#]-111` for quantity and pricing chargebacks, `MCB(yyyymmdd)` for customer-specific promotion billbacks, `[DC#]CNDM(mmmyy)` for concealed damage, `LCBOL(PO#)` for an incomplete bill of lading or packing slip, `AVL(PO#)` for a delivery more than 30 minutes late for its appointment; disputes within 12 months of the deduction, typically resolved in 30 to 45 days: [Glimpse, UNFI and KeHE deductions guide (September 2026)](https://www.tryglimpse.com/post/unfi-kehe-supplier-deductions).

## Inferred

- "Near Asheville": Weaverville is just north of Asheville.
- The online store's share of 2025 revenue (about $235,000). The filing gives only online sales of about $279,000 for January 1 to August 28, 2026, up about 84% on the year before, which puts the same eight months of 2025 near $152,000.

## Invented for this world

The company name, every person, the bank and account numbers, the split of revenue by channel beyond the online estimate, the use of a natural-foods distributor for Whole Foods, QuickBooks Online, Bill.com, Amex, Gusto and Shopify, the airline's procurement portal and its rules, payment terms, every vendor, which debts are taken during 2025 (the second cash advance and the revolving line), and every event in section 6. The filing does not name the company's distributor, systems, bank, lenders or payment terms.

## Known differences from the filing

- The filing's debt table totals $1,138,833, while short-term plus long-term debt on its summary table is $1,071,072. The difference matches the related-party note. The prompt uses the table's individual amounts.
- The real company converted to a Delaware corporation in January 2026. The world ends in 2025, so it stays an LLC.
- The first cash advance's rate is given as "1%, rising to 7% in 2027". The prompt treats it as a holdback on online collections without stating a rate.

## The 60-day version

`cookie_wholesale_60day.md` is the grid's **procure-to-pay, tier 2** cell. It runs 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-01 and moves several of the year's exceptions into those two months: the packaging overrun, the specialty chain's short and double payments, the duplicate butter invoice and new airline portal rejections. It leaves out the fraud, the second advance, the line and the holiday rush. Every moved date is invented, like the originals.
