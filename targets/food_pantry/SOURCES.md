# Sources: Cedar Bend Community Food Pantry

Modeled on the **Coralville Community Food Pantry** in Coralville, Iowa (EIN 47-3509757), from its IRS Form 990 data as published by ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer, and from local news coverage of its building project. The world keeps the real pantry's growth, revenue mix, payroll scale, building project and the rise and fall of its liabilities, and invents the name, people, institutions, account numbers and every dated event.

## Inferred, not stated in the sources

- **What the 2022 liabilities were.** The 990 data give only total liabilities. The world reads the jump to $578,378 at the end of 2022, and its fall over 2023-2025, as a bridge line of credit and the builder's unpaid final bills during construction.
- **Donated food.** The returns include Schedule M (noncash contributions), so part of the contribution total is donated goods. The world treats donated food as passing straight to families without touching a bank account.

## Invented for this world

The pantry's name, every person, the credit union and all institutions, account numbers, the two-signature rule, the matching day, the change order, the bounced check, the gift cards, the cooler repair and every dated event other than the groundbreaking and the move-in.
