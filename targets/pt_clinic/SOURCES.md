# Sources: Lantern Row Physical Therapy

Modeled on **DCPT PLLC**, an employee-owned outpatient physical therapy practice on Capitol Hill in Washington, DC, from its Regulation Crowdfunding campaign page filed on SEC EDGAR (CIK 2004654). Payer mechanics come from Medicare contractors' published rules. The world keeps the real practice's history, size, volume, debts and named challenges, and invents the name, people, account numbers, payers' behavior and every dated event.

## Filings used

| Document | What it gave |
|---|---|
| [Mainvest campaign page, filed December 2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2004654/000174605923000344/dcpt_page.pdf) | Location and 1,200 sq ft clinic; employee-owned; 28 employees and their roles (therapists, PRN therapists, billing manager, patient care coordinators, telehealth and contract therapists, outside CPA); founder's background; milestones (incorporated July 2020, surgery-center contracts August and November 2020, first visit October 2020, first hires December 2020, clinic opened mid-2021 on a 6-year lease, 30th employee December 2023); the joint-replacement service line (pre-op, day of surgery, home, outpatient); $150,000 monthly revenue, $125 average ticket, 60 daily visits; revenue $510,000 (2021), $1,035,000 (2022), $1,535,000 (2023, projected); profitable in Q4 2023; debt of $300,000 from WACIF, lines of credit and short-term debt, and $40,000 cash as of 2023-12-01; challenges (hiring therapists, falling Medicare and private-payer rates); the planned revenue-sharing note (3-7.4% of revenue, quarterly, to December 2026) for a second clinic and refinancing short-term debt |

## Mechanics checked against published rules

- Medicare cannot pay a clean electronic claim before day 14 after receipt (day 29 for paper) and must pay within 30 days: [First Coast, new provider roadmap: claims submission](https://medicare.fcso.com/claims/new-provider-roadmap-claims-submission), [Noridian, clean claims payment and interest](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jadme/claims-appeals/claim-submission/clean).

## Invented for this world

The practice name, every person, the bank and account numbers, the payer list and how each payer behaves, the merchant cash advances (the filing says only "short term debt"), the credentialing delay, the recoupment, the virtual cards, the rate cut at renewal, and every dated event in section 6. The 2% sequestration reduction, Medicare's fee change for 2023 and supplemental-plan crossover are general Medicare facts, not from the filing.
