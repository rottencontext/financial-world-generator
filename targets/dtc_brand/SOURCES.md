# Sources: Kindred Cotton Co.

Modeled on **Tampon Tribe, Inc.**, a Los Angeles maker of organic, plastic-free period products, from its Regulation Crowdfunding filings on SEC EDGAR (CIK 1687465). The world keeps the real company's size, channel mix, financing stack and year-end receivable, and invents the name, people, account numbers, the identity of the institutional buyer and every dated event.

## Filings used

| Document | What it gave |
|---|---|
| [Form C, March 2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1687465/000168746523000002/formcttxo.pdf) | 3 employees; operations since January 2017; the founders' 16 years together and the counter-top start; product line and the small LA warehouse; FY20-FY22 revenue ($987,796, $574,528, $1,145,548), net income and cash; the debt table (convertible notes 2017-2019 at 2-4%, a $100,000 promissory note at 5%, a 2018 crowdfunding convertible note, a 2021 crowdfunding SAFE) |
| [2022 financial statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1687465/000168746523000002/2022combinedfinancials.pdf) | 2022 revenue by channel (DTC $262,550, wholesale $35,673, B2B $849,023), every expense line used in the prompt (contractors $140,743, rent $49,750, Clearco marketing-advance payments, merchant fees, postage), the balance sheet (Citi checking $41,395, receivables $965,325, inventory $244,915, Brex card, zero balances on Wayflyer, Shopify Capital and Fundbox loans, a CBB loan), the cash flow (loan proceeds $164,325, repayments $52,193), and the note that 2021 was kept on a cash basis |

## Mechanics checked against the institutions' own pages

- Shopify Capital repays from a fixed remittance rate applied to each day's sales, with nothing taken on days without sales: [Shopify Capital help](https://help.shopify.com/capital).

## Invented for this world

The company name, every person, the account numbers, the subscription program, the institutional buyer (a school-district purchasing cooperative), the manufacturer's 30/70 payment terms, the timing of each advance, and every event in section 6. The filing shows only that B2B revenue was large and mostly unpaid at year end and that the company used these lenders during 2022.

## Known differences from the filing

The Form C gives FY21 cash as $146,813 while the 2022 cash flow statement opens 2022 at $136,301, probably because 2021 was kept on a cash basis. The prompt uses "about $140,000". The same change of basis makes the 2022 inventory movement in the cash flow statement unreliable, so the prompt leaves inventory amounts out.
