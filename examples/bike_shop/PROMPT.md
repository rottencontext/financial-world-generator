# Spoke & Chain: a one-room bike repair shop

## The company and the time frame

- **Business:** Spoke & Chain, a bike repair shop in Eugene, Oregon, owned and run by Lena Ortiz. It fixes bikes and sells a few parts and accessories. Revenue is about $14,000 a month.
- **Time range:** 2024-03-01 to 2024-03-31, currency USD.
- **History before the start:** the shop opened in 2021. On 2024-03-01 the checking account holds $8,400. The card reader's deposit for February 29 sales ($612.40 before fees) hasn't arrived yet, and one supplier invoice ($620.00, due March 8) is unpaid.

## Institutions (the only sources of data)

- **Willamette Community Bank:** the shop's only account (`Business Checking ••4410`), with a debit card. Shows each posted transaction with a running balance. ACH payments take one business day. Cash deposited at the branch posts the same day.
- **TapPay:** the card reader. Each card sale is recorded with its amount and fee (2.6% + $0.10). Each business day's sales are deposited to checking, minus fees, the next business day. Disputes are pulled back from the next deposit, with a $15 fee.

## The cast

- **Lena Ortiz:** owner and head mechanic. Opens the shop Tuesday to Saturday, pays the bills, and deposits the week's cash on Saturday. Careful with money, but she pays a bill late when cash is tight, and now and then buys lunch on the business debit card by mistake.
- **Theo Banks:** part-time mechanic, 20 hours a week at $22 an hour, paid every other Friday by ACH from checking. Sometimes forgets to ring up a small sale.
- **Customers:** walk-in riders. Most pay by card, some pay cash. A tune-up is $85, a flat fix $20, a full overhaul $250, plus parts.

## Money flows

- **Card sales:** TapPay deposits the previous business day's sales, minus fees.
- **Cash sales:** kept in the till and deposited every Saturday.
- **Parts supplier:** Cascade Cycle Supply ships parts weekly and invoices each shipment ($300 to $900), due in 15 days, paid by ACH.
- **Rent:** $1,800 to the landlord on the 1st, by bank autopay.
- **Payroll:** Theo's pay every other Friday.

## Stories and one-offs

- A customer disputes a card charge, saying the repair didn't fix the problem.
- Lena's Saturday cash deposit is short of what she thought was in the till.
- A shipment from Cascade arrives with a wrong part, and Lena pays the invoice minus that part.
